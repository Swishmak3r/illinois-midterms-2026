# Visual assets

## Illinois editorial illustration

`illinois-editorial.webp` is the optimized website asset. `illinois-editorial.png` is the original generated source. Both are 1254 × 1254 pixels.

Created with the built-in image generation tool for this project. The image is decorative artwork, not a geographic voting-district map. The contour shapes and orange marker must not be used to infer district boundaries, a polling place, or a voter’s location.

Generation brief: a sophisticated tactile paper sculpture of the U.S. state of Illinois, north-up, entire silhouette visible and centered within a square canvas with generous margins. Layered thick cardstock in soft sage and dark forest green, delicately embossed abstract topographic contour patterns, precise cut edges, raised slightly above a warm ivory matte paper background. A tiny burnt-orange decorative marker near northeast Illinois. Palette: ivory #F5F3EC, forest #183C35, sage #D6DED1, copper #C35B3F. Soft daylight from upper left, believable shadows, fine paper fibers, quiet architectural material-study aesthetic. No text, numbers, district boundaries, flags, stars, logos, political symbols, buildings, or people.

The WebP is a format-optimized copy of the generated PNG, with the same dimensions and composition.
