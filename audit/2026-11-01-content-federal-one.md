## Method and limits

Sources were read directly, including official legislative documents and endorser lists. Harrington, Maxwell, and Getty required rendered content; all Getty issue accordions were opened. Exact retrieved source hashes are recorded when available. IFA endorsements remain explicitly labeled primary. Campaign policy proposals are attributed statements, not verified claims about eventual effects.

- Verification checks whether a named campaign, office, legislative record, or endorser actually supports each displayed claim. It does not certify predicted policy effects or campaign rhetoric as objective facts.
- Campaign biographies support attributed self-description; no employer records or professional-license search was performed.
- Primary-era policy pages can establish a stated position but do not prove the candidate has not changed it since publication; Ford entries explicitly note this limitation.
- Hershey’s historical biography verifies background only; current employment is intentionally not asserted.
- Empty issue and hometown fields were excluded; absence of a source does not imply opposition or lack of a position.
- This is a November 1 source snapshot, not a guarantee against later changes. No production files were changed by this auditor.
