## How the check was performed

Five disjoint reviewers inspected the actual pages and documents, while the separate claims audit supplied the seven newly added legislative profiles. Repeated URLs were read once for all relevant claims. The high-use AFL-CIO and Chicago Federation of Labor PDF tables received additional rendered-page inspection. JavaScript-rendered campaign pages and Getty's individual issue accordions were opened when raw HTML contained only headings. Blocked sources were resolved through complete indexed primary text when available, with the access method recorded. A successful HTTP status was never treated as factual verification.

The initial source retrieval covered 236 distinct cited URLs; corrections led to 243 distinct field-evidence URLs in the final production inventory. Reviewers also read supplemental biography and replacement sources. These are source counts, not independent guarantees of truth.

`2026-11-01-content-final.json` is the consolidated exact-current semantic ledger. `2026-11-01-content-verify.cjs` reads production data and fails if a displayed statement changes, lacks a matching supported review, or no longer publishes a reviewed source. It does not refresh external pages. `2026-11-01-content-source-refresh.json` allows review-date updates only for the 530 exact candidate/field/URL combinations actually checked; source publication dates must remain distinct.

Per-scope ledgers retain source URLs, concise supporting paraphrases, access methods and correction history. Raw retrieved pages and temporary browser artifacts are excluded from the deliverable, since they may include irrelevant contact information. The source cache was not used as a substitute for semantic review.

## Boundaries

This content audit does not claim that every candidate has answered every issue, that an empty endorsement list means no endorsements exist, or that every county/write-in contest is represented. Campaign biographies establish sourced or attributed public descriptions; no employer or professional-license background investigation was conducted. The separate complete finance audit covers the 126 state/local filings, 223 federal totals and 38 FEC identities; the early three-sample finance report is not the final finance verification scope. Exact district eligibility remains address-dependent, and sources can change after November 1.
