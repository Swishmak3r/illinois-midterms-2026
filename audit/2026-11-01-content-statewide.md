- Printed/write-in candidacy clauses were checked against the shared SBE extraction and current GPUS list; comprehensive roster and county eligibility reconciliation belongs to the separate roster audit.
- No nonempty issue or endorsement claim remains inaccessible after alternate primary-source access and rendering. The Raoul Blue Book PDF failed in web access; his role was independently corroborated through his current campaign biography and official AG releases.
