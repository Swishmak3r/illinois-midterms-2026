# Cook and collar-county local audit — November 1, 2026

Scope: all 22 existing Cook, DuPage, Lake, Kane and McHenry local races, excluding the separately assigned Third Appellate Court contest. Read-only research; the local-data owner applies changes. This is not a claim of complete local-office coverage or complete write-in coverage.

## Definite corrections

- `cook-sheriff`: Thomas Dart is incorrectly labeled a Challenger in both `role` and `incumbentStatus`. The current [Cook County official directory](https://www.cookcountyil.gov/all-people/thomas-dart) identifies him as Sheriff. Set the status to Incumbent and use an incumbent role for Cook County Sheriff.
- `lake-sheriff`: John D. Idleburg is incorrectly labeled a Challenger in both fields. The current [Lake County elected-official list](https://www.lakecountyil.gov/2473/Elected-Officials) gives John Idleburg the 2022–2026 sheriff term. Set the status to Incumbent and use an incumbent role for Lake County Sheriff.
- Several candidate `role` citations still say that no incumbency claim is made even though Antigravity added one to the role. Candidate rosters support candidacy; add the officeholder sources below separately to substantiate incumbency rather than changing what the roster source proves.
- `dupage-sheriff` and `lake-sheriff` candidate roles retain shortened county office names even though the race title was corrected. Normalize roles to the current full race title.

## Coverage cautions

The data's local research directory remains selective. DuPage's official list also includes county-board, forest-preserve, regional-superintendent and judicial races; Lake and Kane lists contain additional local districts. A passed check of the represented entries does not establish that the site contains every applicable local choice. District-only contests must not be attached to every resident of a county or ZIP.

The prior `audit/local-report.md` refers readers to `local-cook.md` and `local-collar.md`, but those two files do not exist in this checkout.
