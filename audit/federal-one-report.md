# Federal candidate audit: U.S. Senate and Illinois House districts 01–08

Checked October 28, 2026. The assembled file contains 9 races and 21 candidates. The review replaced inherited candidate text with concise primary-source descriptions; missing issue positions are deliberately omitted. Campaign statements establish what a candidate says, not that the candidate’s factual claims or promised outcomes are independently proven.

## Senate and IL-01 corrections

- Corrected Whitfield Harrington Jr.’s ballot party to **American Center Party**, using the Kane County Clerk’s general-election list. Kept his established candidate ID for finance integration. His platform supports direct primary care; the old public-option/universal-care description was unsupported and removed. Replaced invented reproductive-rights, gun and tax specifics with omission.
- Stratton: retained the $25 minimum-wage and Medicare for All proposals, but replaced unsourced SALT/wealth-tax wording with the campaign’s actual million-dollar-income tax proposal. Removed unsupported foreign-policy, specific gun-law, Hyde Amendment and PAC-spending claims. Retained only endorsements read on the endorsers’ own sites: Teamsters Joint Council 25, National Nurses United, EMILYs List and Actors’ Equity. An Obama endorsement was not carried forward without a verified endorsement for this race.
- Tracy: replaced the stock party-platform descriptions with his own affordability and border proposals. His actual energy page includes a clean-energy future; it does not support the discarded categorical anti-clean-energy wording. Removed unsourced reproductive, gun and foreign-policy claims. The Illinois Family Action endorsement is explicitly labeled **2026 primary** because the endorser’s own page says so.
- Jackson: used official congressional biography and dated legislative records. Verified April 2025 Medicare for All cosponsorship through GPO and June 2026 firearm licensing legislation through his official release. The abortion entry is explicitly a 2023 legislative record, not an undated claim. Removed unsupported tax, energy and donor specifics. A search result for a Jackson questionnaire belonged to Jesse Jackson Jr. and was intentionally not used.
- Maxwell: replaced generic Republican policy assumptions with her own current campaign page. Preserved actual price-transparency, maternal-care, legal-immigration, small-business, tax and gun statements. Her pro-life description is supported; detailed abortion restrictions not stated in the retrieved source were removed. Illinois Family Action is labeled as a primary endorsement.
- All inherited dollar amounts and PAC entries were cleared for the separate finance audit. Empty amounts mean unverified here, not zero.
- Unsupported PVI, race ratings, primary vote-share/spending narratives and precise unsupported hometown descriptions were removed. Geographic metadata inherited for integration was not a new boundary certification; ZIP/address lookup is a separate project concern.

## Source and coverage limitations

The Senate roster was read directly on Kane County’s official election website. Campaign and congressional pages that blocked direct access were checked through their indexed first-party text, which can lag live updates. No inaccessible document was represented as having been read.

**IL-07 unresolved roster access:** Koppie’s Republican primary win is verified through official results; final general-election roster status could not be independently retrieved from the election authority. This limitation is stored in race.coverageNote and in the candidate evidence. The record does not assert a complete certified write-in roster.

# Audit: Illinois U.S. House districts 2–4

Checked October 28, 2026. Output: `federal-il02-04.json` (3 races, 8 candidates).

## Scope and decisions

Every inherited candidate biography, issue paragraph, endorsement list, PAC relationship and finance amount in these three races was reviewed. Retained policy summaries are brief paraphrases of the candidate's own campaign or official legislative publication. They document a position, not the truth of campaign talking points. Unsupported specifics were removed instead of inferred from party. No old numeric finance or PAC amount survives; finance validation is a separate integration task.

- **IL-02:** Donna Miller and Michael Scott Noack are active nominees in Illinois State Board of Elections records. Corrected the old claim that Robin Kelly had already vacated her congressional seat: the House directory still lists her as current representative. Removed unsupported primary spending/results narrative and race ratings.
- **IL-03:** Delia Ramirez and Angel Oakley are the two active candidates in the official list. Ramirez's incumbent status is confirmed by her House biography. Removed unsupported rating and primary narrative.
- **IL-04:** The official list includes Patty Garcia, Lupe Castillo, **Ed Hershey (Working Class Party)** and **Chris Getty (Independent)**. Added missing Hershey and matched Getty's displayed name to the official list while preserving existing `christopher-getty` ID. Removed candidates Mayra Macias and Byron Sigcho Lopez are not added. Corrected incumbent wording: Jesús “Chuy” García remains the current officeholder; retirement from reelection is not an already-vacant seat.

## Significant position corrections

- **Noack:** Replaced generic conservative assumptions with his actual platform: nonprofit health insurers, hydroelectric power, and ICE enforcement focused on violent criminals rather than nonthreatening people. Removed undocumented claims of no corporate PAC money, no taxes on tips/overtime/Social Security, abortion stance, blanket firearm-ban repeal and foreign-aid position. His state-tax proposals are labeled as requiring state action.
- **Miller:** Her current campaign explicitly supports a Medicare for All framework, rejoining the Paris Agreement, citizenship pathways and repealing billionaire tax breaks. Removed unsupported claims of unconditional Israel support, NATO/Ukraine positions, South Suburban Airport funding, specific SALT/Child Tax Credit positions and an explicit assault-weapons ban. Her gun page states general stronger-law goals, not that particular bill.
- **Ramirez:** Updated immigration record to her October 13, 2026 Reimagining Safety Act publication. Retained sourced Medicare for All, climate, reproductive, worker-rights and gun positions. Removed unsourced $25 wage specificity, claimed 28% corporate-tax rate and precise PAC receipt percentages. Her campaign's worker-rights page mentions an older $17 bill; the rewrite states a higher minimum wage without implying this is the latest legislative amount.
- **Oakley:** Replaced invented detailed positions with the actual campaign's free-market, low-tax, pollution-reduction, localized-energy and border-reform priorities. Removed unsupported Medicare-for-All opposition, tort reform, guaranteed catastrophic coverage, TCJA, natural-gas/nuclear, abortion, foreign-aid and firearm-policy specifics.
- **Patty Garcia:** Retained current campaign positions on Medicare for All, the PRO Act, citizenship/abolishing ICE, clean energy, expanded Child Tax Credit and Gaza policy. Removed unsupported $25 wage amount, Hyde-repeal, specific gun-ban and corporate-stock-buyback taxation claims.
- **Castillo:** Retained only primary-sourced spending, border, Second Amendment and tips/overtime tax positions. Removed unsupported abortion, foreign-aid, energy, healthcare and PAC assertions. No voter-fraud claim is repeated as fact.
- **Getty:** Read the full expanded platform from the website's embedded page data (the initial text extraction hides accordion content). Replaced broad/invented descriptions with stated earned-legal-status, Dreamer, domestic oil/gas plus renewables, background-check and abortion/IVF positions.
- **Hershey:** Added official candidate identity and historical teaching/union background. The 2026 campaign explicitly says candidates adopted the linked program; only its wage/indexation and antiwar positions are summarized. No party-only inference is used.

## Endorsements

Retained only endorsements found on the endorsing organization's own source: EMILYs List for Miller; Chicago Teachers Union for Ramirez; BOLD PAC for Garcia; Teamsters Joint Council 25 for Miller, Ramirez and Garcia. All other inherited endorsements were omitted as unconfirmed in this audit. An empty list means no endorsement verified here, not no endorsements exist. Generic group names and supporter coalitions are not presented as verified endorsers.

## Source access and limitations

Illinois State Board of Elections candidate lists/details were read through indexed official-page results; direct fetches returned 403. Evidence links point to those exact official records, not search pages. Party, name, office and active status were visible in those results. Current officeholders were cross-checked with the official House directory.

Campaign policy URLs, official House publications and endorser releases were read directly. Getty's full accordion text and Ramirez's October 13 release were also retrieved as HTML when the text browser did not expose all content. Evidence arrays identify the field supported by each source and the check date.

This audit does not certify district boundary metadata. Existing county/community context is preserved as approximate lookup context; the user-facing exact-address election-authority lookup remains necessary. The rewrite is a sourced subset rather than a complete inventory of every candidate's views or endorsements.
