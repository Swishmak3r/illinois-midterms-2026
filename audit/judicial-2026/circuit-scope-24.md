# Twenty-Fourth Circuit retention geography

Checked November 1, 2026. No correction is indicated for James W. Campanella or Chris Hitzemann: the site's Monroe, Perry, Randolph and Washington county set matches the published 2026 election records.

Both judges appear as separate retention questions in every current circuit county's official specimen: [Monroe, PDF page3](https://www.mococlerk.com/files/2026%20GE%20Publication%20Ballot.pdf), [Perry, page2](https://perrycountyil.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/2026-General-Election-Sample-Ballot.pdf), [Randolph, page2](https://randolphcountyclerk.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2026-GE-RANDOLPH.pdf), and [Washington, page2](https://washingtonco.illinois.gov/wp-content/uploads/PUBLICATION_2026_General.pdf). Perry's scanned page was downloaded, rendered and visually read. The others have readable text layers.

The [East St. Louis official specimen](https://eslelections.illinois.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/November-2026-Election-Publication_Specimen-Ballot.pdf), from an election jurisdiction in St. Clair County, has only Barberis in its retention section. It does not include either circuit judge. This corroborates the new four-county electorate rather than the old five-county Twentieth Circuit. It is not represented as an examination of every St. Clair precinct ballot.

The source distinction matters. [Public Act102-0380](https://www.ilga.gov/Legislation/publicacts/view/102-0380) created the division effective December5,2022 and moved the four resident judgeships into the new Twenty-Fourth Circuit. Its section37 allows a qualifying incumbent to seek retention in the original or newly created circuit. Thus a current administrative map cannot, by itself, establish retention scope. The conclusion here rests on the current official publication ballots as well as the law.

No signed individual retention declaration was obtained. Full source URLs, page references, access limitations and the scanned Perry PDF hash are in `circuit-scope-24.json`. No production files were modified.
