# Circuit-retention geographic review — November 1, 2026

The existing county arrays for all **128 circuit-retention questions match the statutory circuit counties**. No production correction is required from this review. Appellate-retention boundaries, including Knecht's older Fourth District, were reviewed separately and must not be inferred from this circuit result.

Illinois Constitution Article VI section 12(d), repeated in [10 ILCS 5/7A-1](https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ILCS/details?ActID=170&ActName=Election+Code.&ChapAct=10+ILCS+5%2F&Chapter=&ChapterID=3&MajorTopic=&SeqEnd=44400000&SeqStart=44200000), places circuit retention elections in the circuit. [705 ILCS 35/1](https://www.ilga.gov/Legislation/ILCS/Articles?ActID=1835&ActName=Circuit+Courts+Act.&ChapAct=705%26nbsp%3BILCS%26nbsp%3B35%2F&ChapterID=50&ChapterName=COURTS) supplies those counties. A residence requirement or initial election in a county/subcircuit does not by itself restrict the later retention electorate to that smaller unit. The [Eighteenth Circuit provision](https://ilga.gov/documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/070500350K2f-18.htm) makes the at-large retention rule explicit for subcircuit judges.

Will's four judges therefore appear countywide: the Twelfth Circuit is Will County. Their original subcircuits are historical election context, not filters for the 2026 retention questions. Cook likewise forms one circuit, so its circuit-retention judges are not restricted to the subcircuit in which they originally ran.

The important exception examined was the 2022 split of the Twentieth Circuit. Section 37 preserves an old/new-circuit choice for qualifying incumbents, so current circuit boundaries alone were insufficient. The companion **circuit-scope-24.json/.md** records actual 2026 official specimens: Monroe, Perry, Randolph and Washington each list both James W. Campanella and Chris Hitzemann; East St. Louis, in St. Clair, lists neither. That supports the site's current four-county Twenty-Fourth Circuit scope.

The [Winnebago official November 3 specimen](https://wincoil.gov/media/attachments/2026/10/03/sample-ballot.pdf), PDF pages 3 and 7, additionally confirms Fabiano, Gulley and White as Seventeenth Circuit retention questions, without the subcircuit restriction shown for separate vacancy contests.

The machine-readable review records every candidate ID, county array and comparison result: **128 match, zero mismatch**. This is a legal geography check with sampled election documents, not a claim to have opened every precinct ballot or certified anyone's personal voting eligibility. Some ILGA direct pages returned 403/502; their current official indexed full sections were readable and are identified as such in the ledger. No names, ratings or rulings were altered.
