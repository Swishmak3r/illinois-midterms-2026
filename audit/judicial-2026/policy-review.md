The party and Trump comparisons do not meet the same-question gate for an affirmative alignment label. The approved contextual entries in `normalizedapprovedpolicycontext.json` still give readers concrete information:

Smith instead involved simultaneous cocaine possession and an operational firearm under Illinois law. No decision applying Hemani to Smith was verified.
