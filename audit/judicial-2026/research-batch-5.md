# Batch 5 judicial-ruling research

Checked November 1, 2026. Verified 16 assigned retention judges against 19 selected rulings using official Illinois court opinions and orders. Every assigned candidate ID matches the inventory. Each profile distinguishes the judge's ruling from appellate outcomes and identifies source paragraphs.

This is selected-case research, not an exhaustive career study or partisan ranking. Individual detention, sentencing, worker/employer or defendant/prosecution outcomes are not treated as ideology proxies.

## Review-sensitive findings

- Vaughan Accuracy Firearms was interim relief and the opinion was later vacated; it is historical, not operative precedent.
- Bohm Kloeppel was reversed by the appellate court; the Illinois Supreme Court unanimously affirmed the reversal in 2022.
- Maher McGauley reversed a first-stage postconviction dismissal; it did not decide innocence or the ultimate merits.
- Brandmeyer Burchell was affirmed on different statutory grounds from the trial judge's stated reasoning.
- Ruth Azcon rationale was checked against paragraph37 of the special concurrence, which quotes his trial order.
- People v. Nettles was excluded from individual Siemer/Brandmeyer profiles because the caption names both and the text did not clearly allocate their separate hearings.
