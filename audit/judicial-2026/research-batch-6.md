The ledger preserves consequential limits: Grischow's temporary workplace vaccination/testing order was vacated; Noll's slating-law injunction protected the named plaintiffs in the 2024 election, and the Supreme Court dismissed the appeal for lack of four votes without creating precedent; Lorton's FOID-records disclosure order was initially affirmed but later reversed on supervisory remand; Rhoades's Zillow appeal was dismissed as premature, and later county minutes establish approval of a proposed settlement rather than execution.

Doscotch's Roa ruling was affirmed on a different rationale. His Wallace decision denied a specific felon-in-possession constitutional challenge; that alone does not establish a general gun-policy agenda or a matched party/Trump position. Mack's referendum ruling was not a voter-ID or vote-access ruling. Procedural criminal rulings and individual detention findings were not converted into ideological labels.
