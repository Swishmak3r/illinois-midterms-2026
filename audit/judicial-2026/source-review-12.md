# Independent primary-source review: batches 1 and 2

Checked November 1, 2026. **Complete: 45 selected cases for 29 judges.** All included primary opinions were read in full by the independent review team; all source PDF hashes match the research ledgers. Every canonical candidate/case pair is covered.

The historical summaries are supported after the corrections below. No unresolved publication blocker remains in this bounded review. This is not a complete career audit, a live docket certification, or a comprehensive current-law citator.

## Corrections applied and verified

- **People v. Simon:** The petition did not allege State misconduct; avoid implying a separate factual finding that no State misconduct occurred.
- **People v. Johnson:** The relevant opinion ends at paragraph 20, not 21.
- **People v. Saucedo:** Separate concurrence pinpoint extends through paragraphs 62-67.
- **Beneficial Illinois Inc. v. Parker:** The trial record does not separately explain the failure-to-honor-rescission counterclaim ruling; footnote 2 belongs with paragraph 16, not paragraph 9.
- **In re Marriage of Durdov:** Public Act 102-0823 amended 750 ILCS 5/510(a)(1), effective May 13, 2022, restricting the contemplation/foreseeability defense to expressly specified events in the court order or incorporated agreement. The 2021 ruling remains history, not current general law.
- **Glorioso v. Sun-Times Media Holdings, LLC:** Public Act 104-0431 expanded press protections; effective August 21, 2025, amendments apply to actions filed on or after January 1, 2026. This does not reverse the 2024 decision or decide its existing suit.
- **People v. Rainey:** Disposition pinpoint corrected to paragraphs 37-38.
- **In re K.M.:** Social-media constitutional holding pinpoint expanded through paragraph 67.
- **People v. Rivera:** Refusal to review continued detention is the precise action; avoid conflation with reconsidering initial detention.

## Attribution and scope

The review distinguishes a trial judge's own action from an appellate panel's reasoning; jury findings from judicial rulings; procedural dispositions from merits affirmances; and a remand from a final award of relief. Sources do not support overall partisan or Trumpism scores.

All 20 batch 1 cases (195 PDF pages) and all 25 batch 2 cases were independently read. The detailed JSON lists every case, its official source, source hash, reviewer and verification notes.

Later-law additions for Durdov and Glorioso were verified in visible limitations and evidence fields so readers do not mistake historical rules for current general law. Unknown later proceedings remain explicit.
