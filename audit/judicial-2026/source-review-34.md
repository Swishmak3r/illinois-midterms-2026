# Independent source review: judicial batches 3 and 4

Completed November 1, 2026. **All 42 included cases were checked against primary court records**, covering 27 of 30 profiles. All 15 historical nomination records in batch 4 were verified against the official 2020 Chicago canvass. There are no pending corrections or unresolved material source errors in this review.

Batch 3 contains 22 cases for 13 judges; batch 4 contains 20 cases for 14 judges. Review covered identity and judicial role, each judge’s own action and stated reason, appellate outcome, trial and appellate dates, source pinpoints, and procedural limits. Batch 4’s independent detail report also records every PDF hash and historical nomination check.

Five corrections were completed and verified:

- **People v. Hicks:** The insufficient-record argument belonged to the State. The appellate court found the record sufficient and rejected the as-applied challenge on its merits.
- **People v. Romero:** The unsupported hearing-deadline waiver argument was forfeited without a merits determination.
- **Georgakopoulos v. Blake:** May 15 was the effective eviction date, not a verified order-entry date.
- **People v. Wordlow:** The source pinpoint ends at paragraph 53, not 54.
- **Lewis v. Kalbhen:** The injunction count was affirmed on forfeiture; the privacy and driver-record claims were resolved on their merits.

Maloney, Mulroe and Reaves-Harris have explicit case-example gaps. Additional Cook searches found no new attributable ruling for Maloney or Mulroe. Those bounded research results do not establish that the judges have no rulings.

The records support factual case summaries, dated nomination history and specifically sourced public statements. They do not support numeric or categorical personal Republican, Democratic or Trumpism ratings. This review is not an exhaustive career review or a complete audit of later appeals and remand proceedings.
