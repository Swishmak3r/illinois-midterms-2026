## Limits

The reviewers read primary claim-bearing text and relevant context rather than treating search hits or captions alone as proof of a judicial act. A published decision's final/slip/released label is distinct from the finality of litigation. Unlocated later proceedings remain explicitly unknown. Historical party claims, when present, require their own dated source; a case result does not establish current affiliation or support for Trump.
