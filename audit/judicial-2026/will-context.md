# Will County retention context — verified November 1, 2026

The [2026 joint ISBA/Will County Bar advisory poll](https://www.isba.org/judicial-elections/counties/will) recommends all four judges. Recommendation percentages: Bertani-Tomczak 87.34 (81 respondents), Jones 70.15 (70), Kennedy 90.22 (92), Rippy 85.37 (83). These are responding lawyers' assessments, not institutional ISBA evaluations or ideology scores. Full metrics and provenance are in `will-context.json`.

The [Will County Clerk's 2014 candidate roster](https://assets01.aws.connect.clarityelections.com/Assets/Connect/RootPublish/will-il.connect.clarityelections.com/Elections/20141104/2014%20General%20Judicial%20Candidates.pdf), page 1, lists Kennedy as Democrat and Rippy as Republican. This supports the narrow statement that each ran under that party in 2014. Their 2026 retention questions are nonpartisan. The PDF was downloaded and read directly because the browser parser received a JavaScript challenge. Private residential addresses have been omitted.

The [November 4, 2014 ISBA report](https://www.isba.org/barnews/2014/11/04/results-tuesdays-judicial-elections-all-circuits-outside-cook) corroborates the parties. Do not confuse the unparenthesized `R` rating (Recommended) with the parenthesized `(R)` party (Republican).

These records do not establish the judges' present political beliefs, a Trump endorsement, or a reliable numerical political score. Case records should retain each judge's exact trial-level role and distinguish the appellate court's subsequent ruling.
